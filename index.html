The Problem

Why It Matters

Agricultural waste like rice husks and coconut fibers is often burned or discarded — causing pollution and wasting potential. At the same time, students and artists depend on expensive, non-biodegradable materials.

Farm by-products are undervalued, eco-friendly options are limited, and communities lose income opportunities. This affects farmers, artists, manufacturers, environmental groups, and consumers alike.

The issue stems from unsustainable art materials and low awareness and demand for recycling agricultural waste.

We're driven by our passion for art, sustainability, and community impact. By transforming agricultural waste into eco-friendly art materials, we reduce environmental harm while supporting farmers and empowering creators.
