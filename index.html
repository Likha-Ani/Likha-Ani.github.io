What If Waste Had a Purpose?

Where does rice straw go after harvest?

Most of it gets burned or left to rot — polluting the air and going to waste. But does that make it truly useless?

Not for Likha-Ani!

The Philippines produces 11–18 million tons of rice straw yearly — and we saw an opportunity. While artists struggled with expensive, non-eco-friendly art materials, we turned that agricultural "trash" into high-quality, affordable canvas paper that's good for your art and the planet.

One farmer's waste. Every artist's new favorite canvas.
